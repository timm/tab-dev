# LLMs and science: the two poles

Reading notes for the tab paper's framing (SP&E draft, docs/tab.tex).
Local pdfs: birhane23science.pdf (here); messeri24illusions.pdf (todo).

## 1. The skeptical frame: Messeri & Crockett, Nature 2024

**Core argument:** AI promotes *illusions of understanding*,
exploiting human cognitive biases to make us believe we understand
the world better than we do, while narrowing the scope of inquiry.

Phases of AI use, each with its epistemic trap:

- **AI as Oracle — illusion of explanatory depth.** Trusting an
  AI's conclusions without the causal mechanism; predictive power
  mistaken for explanation.
- **AI as Surrogate — illusion of objectivity.** Simulating human
  behavior/data and mistaking the training data's systemic biases
  for neutral observed reality.
- **AI as Quant — illusion of comprehensiveness.** Because AI can
  process vast data, we believe it covers all ground; in truth it
  drives science toward the quantifiable, abandoning qualitative
  frontiers.

**Monoculture risk:** when everyone uses the same foundation
models and frameworks, science loses the intellectual diversity
that sparks paradigm shifts.

## 2. The socio-technical critique: Birhane et al., Nat Rev Phys 2023

**Core argument:** LLMs are not neutral calculators; they are
socio-technical systems that centralize power, degrade the
information ecosystem, and institutionalize past bias as objective
future truth.

- **Epistemic pollution:** plausible but wrong scientific text at
  volume, overwhelming peer review and literature search.
- **Homogenization of thought:** most-probable-next-token
  generation suppresses outlier ideas and unconventional theories.
- **Resource and power inequity:** compute costs concentrate the
  steering of science in a few wealthy institutions and monopolies.

## 3. The autonomous counter-pole: Lu et al. (The AI Scientist), 2024

**Core argument:** fully autonomous discovery is here — Oracle and
Surrogate pushed to the limit and celebrated as a feature.

- **End-to-end automation:** brainstorms, codes, runs experiments,
  plots, writes the paper, reviews itself.
- **The philosophical shift:** where the skeptics see erosion of
  the human element, this pole sees the human as a bottleneck; if
  output is novel, reproducible, verifiable, the "illusion" of
  human understanding matters less than empirical velocity.

## Synthesis: a clash of epistemologies

| Metric | Skeptical / socio-technical pole (Messeri, Crockett, Birhane) | Autonomous pole (Lu et al.) |
|---|---|---|
| Goal of science | deep, causal human comprehension | prediction, novel artifacts, output velocity |
| Role of the human | the essential anchor; only seat of understanding and stewardship | a bottleneck to integrate, bypass, or simulate |
| Nature of AI output | a mirror of historical data and human bias | emergent algorithmic creativity, objective synthesis |
| Primary risk | epistemic bankruptcy: more "facts", less understood reality | stagnation from guardrails and distrust of autonomy |

**Where tab sits:** optimize neither for velocity nor autonomy but
for veracity. The LLM is a sounding board and assistant; a small
symbolic artifact (golden tests, speed gates, benchmarked minima)
is the referee that keeps the Oracle honest. Many fast probes,
aggressive deletion, everything checkable by a stranger.
